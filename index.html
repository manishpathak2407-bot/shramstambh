⚖️ Know Your Statutory Rights (कामगारों के कानूनी अधिकार)

Core protections guaranteed under Indian labour laws for unorganized and migrant workers.

5 Core Statutory Legal Protections

  • Maximum 8-Hour Workday: Under the Factories Act and BOCW rules, work beyond 8 hours daily requires overtime compensation at twice the normal rate (2x wages).
  • Mandatory Weekly Rest Day: Every worker is legally entitled to 1 full paid rest day after 6 consecutive days of work.
  • Passbook & Attendance Slip: Section 17 of the Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979 makes it mandatory for contractors to issue written wage passbooks to every laborer.
  • Zero Document Confiscation: Withholding a worker's original Aadhaar, ration card, or voter ID is a criminal offense under IPC Section 340.
  • Safety Equipment & Drinking Water: Helmets, safety boots, first-aid, and clean potable drinking water are non-negotiable statutory duties of the employer on all construction sites.

Key National Labour Enactments

  • Minimum Wages Act, 1948: Mandates legal floor rates that cannot be undercut by any informal contract.
  • Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Act, 1996: Regulates safety, health, accident compensation, and cess funds.
  • Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979: Requires licensing of contractors and protects laborers migrating across state lines.
  • Code on Wages, 2019: Universalizes floor wages across informal day-labor sectors in India.
